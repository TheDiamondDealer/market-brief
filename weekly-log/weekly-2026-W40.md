## CROSS-CURRENTS

1. **Front-end relief did not reach the long end.** Payrolls, wages and core PCE lowered October hike odds by roughly 48 percentage points, yet the 10-year yield rose 4 bp Monday-to-Friday. Fiscal supply, real yields, term premium and energy now carry independent weight.
2. **Equities and gold processed the same payroll print differently.** Nasdaq accepted the pause signal; gold could not hold its initial rally because real yields and DXY stayed high. That is a positioning and discount-rate divergence, not a clean risk-on/risk-off story.
3. **Australia tightened into imported inflation and housing weakness.** The RBA’s fourth hike and 4.0% CPI confirm domestic stagflation risk; ASX’s flat net move hides a temporary Wednesday threshold exit followed by a sharp Thursday reversal.
4. **China’s output signal did not become a commodity-demand signal.** PMI just above 50 was driven by resumed production and AI/export strength; iron ore, lithium, nickel and AUD denied a broad reflation reading.
5. **Energy remained regionally split.** Brent, TTF and thermal coal rose while WTI and Henry Hub fell. The shortage is still concentrated in Gulf/product/LNG channels rather than a uniform global energy beta.
