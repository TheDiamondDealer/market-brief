1. The physical shock did not go away: Brent finished **+2.7%** and WTI **+3.3%**, while sparse shipping and product tightness survived a violent de-escalation rally.
2. The Fed held 9–3, growth slowed and DXY fell **1.6%**, yet the official US 10Y yield rose **10 bp to 4.75%**—the curve, not the dollar, carried the stagflation message.

5. **The single most important thing ahead is Friday’s US jobs report:** it decides whether the long-end/hawkish leg re-accelerates or whether the regime begins losing its policy confirmation.

### The three themes that mattered

#### 1) The energy de-escalation was price noise; impaired physical flows were signal

The US–Iran strike pause triggered a sharp Monday oil fall, but the repair conditions never arrived. By Friday Brent was back at $90.12; only a small number of VLCCs had exited Hormuz, Bab el-Mandeb traffic remained impaired, and Exxon/Chevron warned that fuel tightness could persist into the second half. China’s 14–15% retail fuel-cap increase made the inflation transmission explicit. **Signal:** routes, products and inventories. **Noise:** the day-to-day crude whipsaw.

#### 2) The policy mix split three ways

The Fed’s 9–3 hold and softer GDP/headline PCE pulled DXY lower, but strong private demand and a 0.9% ECI kept the long end under pressure. The BOJ held at 1% by 8–1, warned underlying inflation may overshoot and left September tightening live. Then reported coordinated Japanese and US yen buying produced the sharpest FX move. The regime therefore remains stagflationary, but its old expression—higher DXY, higher USD/JPY, lower everything risky—no longer fits.

#### 3) AI concentration outran the weak broad-demand signal

Microsoft’s earnings-driven surge lifted the Nasdaq even as Apple fell and China’s official manufacturing, non-manufacturing and new-orders indices all moved below 50. High-tech Chinese production remained the positive pocket. The cross-current is important: AI capex can keep copper, selected miners and US indices firm while the broader China/bulk demand impulse weakens.

## CROSS-CURRENTS

1. **China demand → iron ore/copper → AUD/ASX.** Weak official orders argue against broad cyclicals, while AI/grid spending can support copper and selected miners independently. Do not use copper strength alone as proof of a China recovery.
2. **BOJ/yen → carry → global risk.** Policy-supported yen strength is stabilising if leverage adjusts without equity/credit damage; it becomes disorderly only if USD/JPY <160 persists alongside falling equities and wider spreads.
3. **Energy → inflation → central banks.** Crude at $90, tight products and Australia’s 1.3% q/q PPI keep the inflation impulse live even with softer US headline PCE.
4. **AI capex → indices versus breadth.** Microsoft-led index gains can coexist with weaker underlying demand and higher real yields. Index level without breadth is a fragile confirmation.
5. **Treasury supply → global duration.** The 10Y rose despite a falling dollar and softer headline growth. The coming refunding announcement can transmit directly into gold, Nasdaq, AUD/JPY and ASX duration-sensitive sectors.

## CONVICTION SHIFTS — ANALYSIS, NOT ADVICE

### More compelling

- **Physical energy stress over crude headline direction.** The week validated routes, product cracks and inventories as the durable signal.
- **Long-end pressure independent of DXY.** The 10Y rose 10 bp while DXY fell 1.6%; the funding/term-premium channel deserves more weight.
- **Selective AI capex resilience.** Earnings proved it can overpower macro weakness at index level, at least temporarily.
- **Yen as a policy-managed cross-asset variable.** US participation, if confirmed, changes the intervention reaction function even without a carry unwind.

### Less compelling

- **One-way dollar-up risk-off.** DXY <100 and USD/JPY <160 invalidated that expression.
- **Headline Nasdaq as broad risk confirmation.** Microsoft concentration and a still-active sub-25,900 trigger weaken the signal.
- **A single gold close as a regime verdict.** Conflicting late Friday marks around $4,050 require follow-through.
- **Copper/ASX strength as proof of China recovery.** Official orders contracted; AI and supply factors can create false positives.
