## CROSS-CURRENTS

1. **Physical inflation strengthened while market inflation compensation eased.** Brent, TTF and Henry Hub rose sharply, but the 10-year breakeven fell about 3 bp as auction demand lowered both nominal and real yields.
2. **Long-end disorder found price-sensitive demand.** The 10-year’s 5.3645% high triggered no broad liquidation; successive auctions cleared strongly enough to pull the curve lower. That moderates, but does not erase, the duration regime.
3. **The Fed’s path and the Treasury term premium stayed separate.** Minutes and Waller preserved another-hike optionality while allowing October to pass; front-end odds fell even as long yields stayed historically high.
4. **China reopened into a two-speed commodity tape.** Copper held structural strength while iron ore collapsed on weak mill margins and hot-metal demand. Lithium and nickel did not confirm reflation.
5. **Equities remained a poor binary risk gauge.** The S&P advanced and Nasdaq stayed above its invalidation despite high yields and weak consumer sentiment; leadership remains narrow enough that CPI can still force a rapid repricing.
