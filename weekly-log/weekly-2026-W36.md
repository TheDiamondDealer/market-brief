1. Renewed US–Iran fighting re-priced the physical shock: Brent closed at **$96.28 (+6.6%)** and WTI at **$91.48 (+7.0%)**, while Hormuz transit remained impaired.
2. August payrolls rose **162,000** against a **56,000** consensus, taking September hike odds back to about **58%** after Thursday’s Waller-led drop below 50%.

5. The next decisive test is US CPI on **Friday 11 September at 22:30 AEST**: hotter core inflation would validate the oil/payroll policy channel; a soft print would reopen the stabilisation path.

## CROSS-CURRENTS AND DIVERGENCES

1. **Oil up, dollar down:** The energy shock lifted inflation compensation and yields without restoring the one-way DXY surge. That is stagflationary pressure, but not the prior disorderly dollar squeeze.
2. **Yields high, US indices resilient:** Nasdaq’s close above 26,400 invalidated the live bearish trigger even as the 10-year stayed near 4.8%. Semiconductor strength and concentration remain the bridge.
3. **Yen stronger, no broad deleveraging:** USD/JPY’s break below 160 removed the weak-yen trigger. The missing equity/credit follow-through argues against calling a confirmed carry unwind.
4. **Seaborne iron ore up, Dalian flat below 740:** China’s physical signal improved only at the margin; copper’s flat week and sub-50 manufacturing backdrop keep the impulse bifurcated.
5. **Brent up, ASX resource equities down:** Materials’ roughly 5% weekly decline and energy’s Friday weakness show that equity investors are discounting margin, policy or demand risk rather than treating the commodity move as clean earnings upside.
6. **Gold resilient, but not a haven override:** Gold ended near its Monday reference, yet the adverse Tuesday and Friday rate reactions show the live real-yield framework still governs at high-frequency horizons.

## REGIME DECISION

**No live regime-state amendment is warranted.** The week strengthened the physical-energy and policy-restriction core already embedded in the live acute-stagflation frame. It did not meet the stronger disorderly-risk-off conditions: DXY remained below 100, USD/JPY fell below 160, US indices did not break down, and a broad credit-stress confirmation was not verifiable.

Re-strengthening of the disorderly expression would require a joint move closer to the live rule set: Brent above $100 and/or the US 10-year above 4.75% **with** DXY moving through 101–101.5, weaker equity breadth and observable credit deterioration. Stabilisation still requires durable energy-flow repair with Brent below $85, the 10-year below 4.45%, DXY below 100 and Nasdaq above 26,400. Only the last two conditions are currently satisfied.
