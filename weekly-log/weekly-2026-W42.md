## REGIME DASHBOARD

- **Regime:** **ACUTE STAGFLATION INTACT; THE PHYSICAL AND PRODUCER-PRICE CORE STRENGTHENED; DURATION DISORDER EASED AT THE MARGIN; THE AI CONCENTRATION FRACTURE DEEPENED, BUT BROAD DISORDERLY RISK-OFF IS NOT FULLY RESTORED — SHIFTING.**
- **Change this week:** **Energy and pipeline inflation re-accelerated while sovereign yields rallied.** Brent rose 3.0% Monday-to-Friday to $109.86, WTI 8.2% to $101.41 and TTF 3.8% to €87.55. September US CPI rose 0.6% m/m and 3.7% y/y; PPI rose 1.3% m/m and 6.3% y/y. Against that, the official US 10-year fell 15 bp from the prior Friday to 5.09% and its real yield fell 17 bp to 2.74%.
- **Risk tone:** **Bifurcated / rotation rather than broad liquidation.** Nasdaq fell 4.1% Monday-to-Friday and 4.5% Friday-to-Friday as the AI-financing trade cracked, but roughly 70% of S&P 500 constituents gained, the equal-weight index rose and the Dow gained 0.7% on the week.
- **Volatility:** **High in technology, energy and lithium; lower in sovereign yields by week-end.** CoreWeave fell 25% on the week, the semiconductor index recorded its longest losing streak since 2008, WTI finished above $100 and battery-grade lithium fell 11.5% between the tracked endpoints.
- **Gold vs real yields:** **Path confirmed; endpoint beta was muted.** Gold sold off sharply when CPI lifted yields on Wednesday, then recovered as nominal and real yields fell. It finished almost flat Monday-to-Friday despite a 17 bp decline in the real 10-year, so the inverse relationship held intraday but did not deliver strong weekly convexity.
- **Oil vs inflation expectations:** **Partially confirmed.** Oil and TTF strengthened and the 10-year breakeven rose about 2 bp to 2.35%; the expectations move was small relative to the physical shock because nominal and real yields both rallied on defensive demand.
- **Yen carry health:** **Stretched but not unwinding.** USD/JPY eased only 0.1% to 158.01 and remained below the old 160 invalidation; AUD/JPY fell 0.3% to about 109.77. There was no broad carry liquidation.
- **China demand impulse:** **Export-strong, domestic-demand weak and commodity confirmation absent.** September exports rose 36.3% y/y and imports 31.0%, while CPI rose only 0.9% and core CPI 1.0%. Copper fell 2.5%, lithium 11.5% and nickel 1.7%; 62% iron ore was flat and Dalian iron ore remained below CNY740.
- **Price-confirmation score:** **4 aligned / 2 diverged / 0 too early.** Hot US CPI, weak Australian employment, Friday's renewed Gulf escalation and the AI-financing shock produced the expected first-order moves. Hot US PPI/retail sales failed to sustain higher yields, and China's export surge failed to lift the battery/industrial complex.
- **Complex bias for the coming week:** Precious **mixed, medium** while falling real yields compete with a hawkish inflation path; battery/bulk **mixed-down, high** until China activity and physical demand confirm; energy **upward pressure with gap risk, very high** while Brent is near $110 and TTF near €88; US equities **bifurcated-down, high** with Nasdaq in its live warning zone; ASX **mixed, medium** as weaker RBA pricing offsets commodity stress; USD/rates **two-sided, very high** with DXY still above 101.5 but yields below the prior week's peak. The decisive tests are China's Q3 activity release and whether major technology earnings validate AI cash flows and financing.
