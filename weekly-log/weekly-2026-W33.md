### The themes that mattered

#### 1. Disinflation moved the front end; fiscal and energy risk held up the long end

US July CPI rose 0.1% m/m and 3.4% y/y, with core CPI at 0.2% m/m and 2.5% y/y. PPI was flat m/m and slowed to 4.7% y/y from 5.5%. September hike odds fell from 52% on Monday to roughly one-third by Friday, DXY remained below 100, and Nasdaq held above 26,400. Yet the 10-year yield ended around 4.68% and a 30-year auction cleared at the highest yield since 2001. The signal is a steeper, not uniformly easier, curve: near-term Fed restraint is being priced, but energy and fiscal term premium are not. [US CPI](https://www.reuters.com/business/us-consumer-prices-increase-expected-july-2026-08-12/) [US PPI](https://www.reuters.com/business/us-producer-prices-unchanged-july-2026-08-13/) [Treasury market](https://www.wsj.com/finance/jgbs-rise-tracking-gains-in-u-s-treasurys-52148278)

#### 2. Physical energy stress beat demand downgrades

Brent rose only 0.9% from Monday’s already elevated close, but 6.0% Friday-to-Friday. Renewed tanker attacks, an indefinite US blockade threat and disruption at Russia’s Novorossiysk export terminal outweighed a surprise US crude build and weaker oil-demand projections. TTF held above €60 as heat, Norwegian maintenance, constrained LNG transit and low storage tightened Europe. This was the clearest regime-confirming thread. [Oil close and physical evidence](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-set-weekly-gains-after-us-threatens-indefinite-blockade-iran-2026-08-14/) [OPEC August assessment](https://publications.opec.org/momr) [European gas stress](https://www.ft.com/content/42194267-cda2-4dcc-9487-02c5f239e034)

#### 3. China remained a demand question, not a broad commodity collapse

China’s July CPI slowed to 0.5% y/y and fell 0.1% m/m; PPI slowed to 3.5% y/y and fell 0.7% m/m. The producer details showed fuel/power purchasing prices down 2.6% m/m and ferrous inputs down 0.2% m/m. Iron-ore port inventories rose by 390,000 tonnes, yet iron ore edged up and copper was flat while lithium and coking coal rose. The durable signal is weak domestic demand with asset-specific supply support, not a synchronized China liquidation. [China CPI](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202608/t20260810_1965018.html) [China PPI](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202608/t20260810_1965017.html) [Iron-ore inventories](https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/mmi-daily-iron-ore-index-report-august-14-2026/)

### Regime verdict

**No amendment.** The regime is not broken, because the physical energy shock and long-end rate stress remain live. It is not returning to disorderly risk-off, because DXY, USD/JPY, Nasdaq, AUD/USD and near-term Fed pricing all fail that confirmation test. The best description remains the 31 July state: **acute stagflation intact; disorderly risk-off expression fractured.**

The standing de-escalation cluster also did not fire: Fed-hike odds and DXY met their easing conditions, but Brent did not fall below $76 with repaired flows and the US 10-year did not fall below 4.45%. Two sessions of three-condition confirmation were therefore absent.

## CROSS-CURRENTS

### China demand → iron ore + AUD

The demand data softened, but the yuan and AUD firmed and iron ore rose slightly. The implication is that weak China demand is already partly discounted and is being offset by supply contracting and global dollar softness. It should not yet be read as a China reflation signal.

### BOJ/yen → carry → global risk

The BOJ discussion became more hawkish, but USD/JPY stayed around 159 and AUD/JPY rose. That means carry remains functional under intervention management. A break below 160 alone is not the signal; it needs falling equities and wider credit spreads.

### Oil → inflation expectations → curve shape

Cool CPI/PPI lowered the probability of a near-term Fed hike, while renewed oil stress and a poor 30-year auction kept long yields high. That steepening combination is the cleanest cross-asset expression of this regime: easier front-end expectations, harder long-duration discount rates.

### AI concentration → Nasdaq resilience → commodities divergence

Nasdaq remained above 26,400 despite high real yields and renewed energy stress. That prevents a broad risk-off label, but the ASX decline and palladium weakness show the resilience is narrow rather than universal.
