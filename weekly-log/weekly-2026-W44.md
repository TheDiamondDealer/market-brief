### Dominant themes

1. **The inflation-growth mix became more stagflationary, not recessionary.** US real GDP grew 3.4% annualised in Q3, above 3.2% consensus, while September headline PCE rose 3.6% y/y and 0.5% m/m. Australia CPI reached 4.3% y/y, with quarterly headline at 1.3% and trimmed mean at 1.0%. The policy constraint tightened even as long yields eased late in the week. ([BEA GDP](https://www.bea.gov/index.php/news/2026/gdp-advance-estimate-3rd-quarter-2026); [Reuters PCE](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/us-inflation-heats-up-in-september-consumer-spending-rises-ce785cd3d081f22d); [ABS CPI](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/price-indexes-and-inflation/consumer-price-index-australia/latest-release))
2. **AI shifted from revenue enthusiasm to funding discipline.** Microsoft generated $18.6bn free cash flow while spending $45.2bn on infrastructure; Alphabet spent more than operating cash flow, Amazon posted a $42.4bn trailing cash burn and Intel warned of cash burn. Investors punished unfunded or weak-return capex, pulling Nasdaq below its trigger. ([Reuters AI cash-flow review](https://www.reuters.com/business/cash-is-king-investors-draw-new-battle-lines-ai-spending-boom-2026-10-29/))
3. **Central banks paused, but the global direction remained tighter.** The Fed held 3.75–4.00% with two dissenters seeking a hike; BOJ held 1.25% with one dissenter seeking 1.5%, upgraded inflation and signalled more hikes. RBA Governor Bullock said inflation was too high before next week's decision. ([Reuters Fed](https://www.reuters.com/business/fed-likely-go-into-wait-and-see-mode-rates-2026-10-28/); [Reuters BOJ](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/bank-japan-hold-rates-signal-210126915.html); [Reuters Australia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/823549))
4. **China did not deliver a demand reset.** The four-day plenum's public emphasis was party discipline and anti-corruption rather than an investable fiscal or property-demand package. Iron ore, lithium and nickel remained below live downside thresholds. ([AP plenum review](https://apnews.com/article/64e17fadcad51932f5d320cd4a97cc1f))

- **Confirmed downside:** gold closed below 4,050; Nasdaq closed below 25,900; DXY remained above 101.5; lithium stayed below CNY150,000; nickel below $16,000; Dalian iron ore below CNY740.
- **Confirmed upside/stress:** Brent remained above $110; TTF remained far above €52; uranium closed above $88; ASX 200 closed above 8,830; copper stayed above $6.30 but without required China breadth.
- **De-escalation:** US10Y closed below 5.00, preventing further duration escalation; USDJPY remained below 160/162; AUDUSD remained above 0.700 and well above 0.688.
- **Relationship failure:** Gold fell sharply while real yields eased marginally. This is the opposite of the safe-haven override required for a broad disorderly interpretation.
- **Regime test:** The DXY/Nasdaq price pair fired, but the live rule also requires weak breadth/credit widening or equivalent multi-session equity/credit/carry liquidation. Russell 2000 and ASX resilience, stable yen and N/V credit evidence mean the full condition did **not** complete.

**Live-file decision:** no amendment to `regime-state.md`, `baseline-dossier.md` or `thresholds.md`. The current regime already captures acute stagflation, escalated duration risk and incomplete broad disorder; W44 changes confirmation, not the definition.

## CROSS-CURRENTS

- **Bullish energy / bearish duration should have lifted long yields, but it did not.** The 10-year fell below 5% as Friday's tech shock drew duration demand. This weakens the “one-way inflation liquidation” expression without weakening the physical shock.
- **Bearish precious metals / softer real yields is a meaningful divergence.** It points to liquidation, weak Asian demand and a stronger-for-longer policy narrative rather than classic USD-real-yield mechanics.
- **Nasdaq breakdown / Russell and ASX resilience is rotation, not yet broad capitulation.** The market punished cash-burning AI leaders while rewarding smaller companies and Australian materials.
- **Hot Australian CPI / flat AUD is another warning.** Domestic rate support was cancelled by global risk aversion and a firm dollar.
- **Hawkish BOJ / stable USDJPY keeps carry risk latent.** The trigger can accelerate quickly if December pricing moves from guidance to certainty.
