[Reuters equities](https://www.reuters.com/business/wall-st-futures-rise-us-iran-pause-hostilities-2026-07-27/)
4. **Gold repaired without completing the haven override.** Gold rose toward $4,080 as the dollar softened, even though real yields edged higher. **So what:** the bearish rates relationship is diverging again, but only a close above $4,150—or a sustained fall in real yields—changes the trigger state.
5. **Australia’s market crossed its upside threshold before CPI.** The ASX 200 closed 8,894, reversing Friday’s downside break. **So what:** imported-energy relief can broaden the domestic rally, but Wednesday CPI determines whether RBA pressure replaces the oil shock as the local constraint.
