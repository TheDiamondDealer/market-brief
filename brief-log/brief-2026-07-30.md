## LISTEN

2. **The Fed held, but the 9–3 vote was hawkish under the surface.** Cleveland’s Beth Hammack, Minneapolis’s Neel Kashkari and Dallas’s Lorie Logan wanted a 25 bp hike. **So what:** immediate hike risk is gone, but September remains live around 57–64% and the long end is pricing inflation/term premium.
3. **The curve, not the headline rate, carried the warning.** Two-year yields fell while US10Y rose to 4.67% and 30-year to 5.20%. **So what:** this is not dovish relief—it is a bear-steepening signal that supply shocks and fiscal/term-premium risk remain unresolved.
4. **AI results separated real monetisation from cash burn.** Microsoft rose 4% after Azure grew 43%; Meta fell 10% after free cash flow collapsed 91% to $784 million. **So what:** “AI” is no longer one trade; revenue-to-capex conversion decides winners.
5. **Australia received genuine domestic relief.** June CPI fell 0.1% m/m, annual CPI eased to 3.8%, and Q2 trimmed mean undershot. **So what:** August RBA hike odds collapsed to 3% and the ASX cleared 9,000, but services, housing and renewed oil remain the medium-term inflation caveat.
