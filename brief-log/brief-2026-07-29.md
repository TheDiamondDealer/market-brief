## LISTEN

3. **Gold broke down anyway.** Spot fell 1.2% to $4,026.49 while DXY held near a one-month high. **So what:** dollar positioning and Fed-event risk overrode lower yields; the bearish price trigger fired, but its rates confirmation is incomplete.
4. **Semiconductors have become an independent risk factor.** SOX fell 4.5%, the KOSPI plunged more than 10% and Nasdaq slipped despite lower oil and yields. **So what:** Chinese competition, stretched valuations and AI funding/cash-conversion concerns now matter more than the macro discount-rate relief.
5. **Australia’s rally is broad but not commodity-led.** ASX 200 reached 8,947.8 as banks, discretionary, healthcare, tech and property rallied; materials lost 1.36%. **So what:** today’s CPI tests whether domestic-rate relief can survive without a miners’ cushion.
