# Regime State — November 2026 (DRAFT)

> **AWAITING HUMAN APPROVAL.** Proposed replacement for `regime-state.md`. The live regime remains unchanged.

**Proposed classification:** **SHIFTING**  
**Proposed state:** **ACUTE PHYSICAL-ENERGY AND POLICY STAGFLATION INTACT; DURATION DISORDER EASED BELOW ESCALATION; AI CASH-FLOW/CONCENTRATION FRACTURE ACTIVE; BROAD DISORDERLY RISK-OFF NOT FULLY RESTORED.**

## Executive state

October strengthened the physical and inflation core but weakened the September duration expression. Brent and TTF moved deeper into acute territory, US and Australian inflation re-accelerated, and policy pricing turned more hawkish. At the same time, US 10-year nominal and real yields fell roughly 30bp. The stress is therefore rotating from a pure yield shock into an energy, policy and corporate-cash-flow squeeze.

The old price pair for broad disorder—DXY above 101.5 and Nasdaq below 25,900—was met by month-end. The confirmation cluster was not: smaller companies and ASX equities held up, yen carry was orderly, and observable broad credit widening was not verified. The pair is an alert, not a regime verdict.

## Active pillars

### 1. Physical energy — acute and strengthening

- Brent around $113/bbl; TTF around €103/MWh.
- Gulf crude exports remain below pre-war capacity; refined products and LPG are more impaired.
- Tanker costs, insurance and voyage duration are transmitting scarcity beyond headline crude prices.
- OPEC+ held December production at September required levels; logistics and product conversion remain more binding than quota policy.

### 2. Policy and inflation — hawkish asymmetry

- US September PCE and Australian September CPI accelerated.
- The Fed held with two hike dissents; the RBA faces an immediate 3 November tightening decision.
- Growth remains strong enough to delay relief but not necessarily strong enough to absorb persistent energy inflation.

### 3. Duration — eased, still restrictive

- US 10-year nominal yield fell from about 5.29% to roughly 4.98%; the real yield fell from about 2.93% to 2.63%.
- This breaks the September escalation but does not create an easing regime. A renewed move above 5.10%, then 5.25%, would reactivate the duration leg.

### 4. Equities — selective fracture, not systemic liquidation

- Nasdaq lost about 5.3% in October and ended near 25,437.
- Equal-weight and smaller-company breadth outperformed the cap-weighted index.
- AI capex exceeded aggregate operating cash flow across the largest platforms; free-cash-flow conversion and financing quality are now the active fault line.

### 5. Precious metals — structural support, tactical failure

- Gold fell below $3,900 despite lower nominal and real yields.
- Central-bank buying and Chinese ETF demand remain supportive, but the rates-only relationship has failed tactically.
- A recovery above $4,000 is needed before gold resumes regime-confirming leadership.

### 6. China and commodities — weak domestic demand, split transition complex

- Manufacturing PMI at 49.3 confirms contractionary demand.
- Iron ore, lithium and nickel weakened; copper and uranium remained comparatively resilient.
- Broad policy easing is not a regime change until orders, housing, credit demand and physical premiums respond.

### 7. Yen and carry — elevated, orderly

- USDJPY near 157.5 keeps intervention and imported-inflation risk alive.
- No fast carry unwind is visible. Above 160.5 or below 154 with rising JGB yields are the relevant transition levels.

## Proposed signal hierarchy

1. **Physical:** Brent/TTF plus verified flows, freight and inventory.
2. **Policy:** Fed/RBA/BOJ pricing plus realised inflation.
3. **Financial:** US 10-year nominal/real yields, DXY and credit spreads.
4. **Risk:** Nasdaq, equal-weight/small-cap breadth and carry.
5. **Demand:** China orders, steel margins and physical commodity premiums.

## Escalation and exit conditions

### Restore broad disorderly risk-off if

- Nasdaq closes below **25,200** while DXY holds above **102.2**, **and**
- at least two confirmations appear: weak equal-weight/small-cap breadth, visible IG/HY credit widening, USDJPY below 154 in a fast carry unwind, or simultaneous ASX/cyclical weakness.

### Further escalate acute stagflation if

- Brent closes above **$118**, with **$120+** and further flow impairment the acute override; or
- TTF closes above **€110/MWh**; or
- US 10-year yield records two closes above **5.10%**, with **5.25%+** the duration override; or
- inflation surprises higher while Fed/RBA hike probabilities rise at least 15 percentage points.

### De-escalate toward stabilisation if

- Brent closes below **$105** and TTF below **€90** with verified flow/freight repair; and
- DXY falls below **101.0**; and
- Nasdaq reclaims **26,000** with broader breadth and stable/tighter credit; and
- US 10-year yield remains below **4.75%** without a recessionary credit shock.

### Break the regime outright if

- physical energy flows normalise, inflation rolls over across the US and Australia, and policy easing becomes credible without credit deterioration; or
- a verified broad credit/carry liquidation overwhelms the selective structure and becomes the dominant system state.
