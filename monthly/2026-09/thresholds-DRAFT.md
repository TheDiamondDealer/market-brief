## Trigger protocol

- Use the stated close; an intraday touch is a warning only.
- A boundary breach needs its confirmation condition. Otherwise label it **tested, unconfirmed**.
- “Two closes” means two observations from the same benchmark and contract.
- Less-liquid commodity levels are provider/benchmark specific; verify unit, contract and timestamp before use.
- Market-implied probabilities are not central-bank forecasts.
- Do not turn a scenario condition into a recommendation.

## Relationship triggers

### Precious-metals haven override

- **Warning:** gold and silver rise on a day when Brent, DXY and nominal/real US yields also rise.
- **Headline trigger:** the joint move persists for two closes; gold >$4,650 and silver >$68.
- **Interpretation:** strategic/haven demand has overtaken the rates channel.
- **Invalidation:** gold < $4,500 and silver < $65 while DXY/yields remain high.

### Yen carry unwind

- **Warning:** USD/JPY falls rapidly from above 162 or AUD/JPY approaches 110.
- **Headline trigger:** USD/JPY <156 and AUD/JPY <110 with broad equities down and credit wider.
- **Interpretation:** endogenous carry deleveraging, not benign BOJ normalisation/intervention.
- **Invalidation:** USD/JPY 158–161, AUD/JPY >112 and risk assets stabilise.

### China demand confirmation

- **Upside trigger:** copper >$6.65 and Dalian iron ore >CNY750 for two closes with premiums/imports, hot-metal/margins or credit/infrastructure confirmation.
- **Downside trigger:** copper < $6.30 and Dalian <CNY710 with weaker physical data.
- **Invalidation:** commodities diverge or physical evidence contradicts price.

### Product/LNG-led inflation shock

- **Warning:** Brent is stable/down but TTF and product cracks rise.
- **Headline trigger:** TTF >€72 or product benchmarks make new stress highs with verified storage/refinery/LNG impairment.
- **Interpretation:** consumer-energy inflation can strengthen without a Brent breakout.
- **Invalidation:** TTF <€60 and product cracks normalise with repaired flows.

### AI-concentration failure

- **Warning:** Nasdaq rises while equal-weight/semis fall and credit widens.
- **Headline trigger:** Nasdaq <25,800 with weak semiconductors, breadth and credit while US10Y >4.80%.
- **Invalidation:** Nasdaq >26,800 with broad participation and stable credit.

## Physical confirmation checklist

A price trigger is materially stronger when at least one matches:

- official route, port, refinery, mine or smelter outage/restart;
- export, vessel, inventory, stockpile or storage change;
- exchange spread, premium or stock consistent with the move;
- implemented producer quota/guidance rather than announcement;
- market breadth or credit confirmation;
- central-bank vote/guidance rather than anonymous interpretation.
