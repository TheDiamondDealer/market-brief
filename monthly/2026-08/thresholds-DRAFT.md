## Trigger protocol

- Use closes unless the row explicitly says intraday.
- A boundary breach needs the listed confirmation; otherwise label it “tested, unconfirmed.”
- Two consecutive closes means two observations from the same benchmark/contract.
- Verify contract, unit and timestamp before publishing.
- Market-implied probabilities are not official forecasts.

## Physical confirmation checklist

A price trigger is materially stronger when at least one matches:

- official route/port/refinery outage or restart;
- export, vessel, inventory, stockpile or storage change;
- exchange spread/premium consistent with the move;
- producer quota/guidance implemented rather than announced;
- market breadth or credit confirmation;
- central-bank vote/guidance, not anonymous interpretation.
