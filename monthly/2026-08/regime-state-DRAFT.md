# Proposed Regime State — August 2026

> **DRAFT — AWAITING HUMAN APPROVAL.** Prepared 3 August 2026 AEST. This file does not replace `regime-state.md`.

## Verdict

**SHIFTING — acute energy/long-duration stagflation remains intact, but the disorderly risk-off expression has fractured.**

The physical core has not broken: Brent ended July near $90 after a 24% monthly rise, refined-product cracks remained extreme, the US 10-year closed at 4.75% with a 2.47% real yield, and Fed/RBA/BOJ policy remained restrictive. The previous “strong USD + weak JPY + broad duration sell-off” cluster did break: DXY fell below 100, USD/JPY fell below 160 after reported coordinated intervention, and cap-weighted US equities recovered through a narrow AI earnings impulse.

This is a regime transition in **expression**, not yet a transition to broad disinflation or risk-on.

## What changed from July

- **Energy:** escalated from a risk premium to a physically confirmed crude-and-products shock, then partially eased. OPEC+ added 188 kb/d of September quota but left about 2 mb/d of older cuts intact.
- **Policy:** the Fed’s 9–3 hold included three dissents for a hike; the RBA remained at 4.35%; the BOJ held at 1.0% with one dissent for 1.25%.
- **FX:** DXY broke below 100 and reported US–Japan intervention pushed USD/JPY below 160. This invalidated the old weak-yen expression.
- **Equities:** Nasdaq finished July lower, but Microsoft/Amazon concentration prevented a broad disorderly unwind.
- **China:** July PMIs moved back below 50, while copper and selected transition metals remained supported by supply and structural demand.
- **Gold:** finished July up 1.1% but exactly around the $4,050 boundary; the haven override remains unconfirmed.

## Active transmission map

1. **Energy route/product stress → inflation expectations → Fed/RBA pricing → real yields → duration and gold.**
2. **Reported intervention → lower USD/JPY and DXY.** Do not infer carry deleveraging unless equities fall, credit widens and yen strength persists.
3. **AI cash-flow concentration → cap-weighted index resilience.** Use breadth, equal-weight, semiconductors and credit to test it.
4. **China weakness → iron ore/AUD downside**, unless inventory, mill margins or supply policy offset it.
5. **Supply concentration → nonlinear metals moves.** Indonesia quotas, Simandou ramp, uranium origins and PGM disruptions can dominate aggregate demand.
6. **Long-end term premium → yields higher even with DXY lower.** This is a live sign-flip from the usual dollar/yield bundle.

## What would shift the verdict

### Re-strengthen disorderly stagflation

Any two of the following sustained for two closes, with at least one physical or credit confirmation:

- Brent >$100 or product/logistics impairment worsens.
- US10Y >4.75% and real 10Y >2.50%.
- DXY >101.5.
- USD/JPY >162 after intervention.
- Nasdaq <25,000 with weak breadth, wider credit or semiconductor confirmation.
- Fed September hike probability ≥75%.

### Stabilise into managed stagflation

- Brent $84–92 with improving transit and product cracks;
- DXY 99–101;
- USD/JPY 155–160 without broad deleveraging;
- US10Y 4.45–4.75;
- Nasdaq regains 26,400 with breadth.

### Break toward disinflation / broad risk repair

All required, not cherry-picked:

- Brent <84 for two closes and physical routes/product markets materially repair;
- US10Y <4.45 and real 10Y <2.25;
- Fed hike odds <50%;
- DXY <99 without crisis funding stress;
- Nasdaq >26,400 with breadth and stable/tighter credit.

### Break toward liquidity dislocation

- USD/JPY <155 **with** broad equity decline and wider credit;
- DXY funding stress or basis dislocation;
- simultaneous liquidation in gold, copper and equities;
- impaired market functioning or emergency central-bank liquidity action.

## Monitoring cadence

- Daily: triggers, physical energy confirmation, rates/FX/equity breadth.
- Weekly: whether divergences persist and whether the relationship scorecard changes.
- Monthly: formally reassess only after sustained multi-asset confirmation.
