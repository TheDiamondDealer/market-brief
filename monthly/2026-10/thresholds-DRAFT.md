## Trigger protocol

1. **Observe:** record benchmark, contract, timestamp, currency and source.
2. **Confirm:** apply the confirmation column. Intraday touches remain “on the radar.”
3. **Explain:** state the regime-adjusted direction and affected assets.
4. **Invalidate:** publish the exit condition with the trigger.
5. **Reset:** monthly, after a regime break, benchmark discontinuity, contract roll or >10% structural repricing.

## Relationship triggers

### Precious-metals haven override

- **Warning:** gold closes positive while Brent, DXY and US10Y are also positive.
- **Trigger:** joint move persists two sessions and gold closes above $4,300.
- **Confirmation:** ETF/physical/official demand strengthens and silver participates.
- **Invalidation:** gold closes below $4,200 while real yields/DXY remain high.

### Yen carry unwind

- **Warning:** USD/JPY drops >1.5% intraday from above 160 or closes below 155.
- **Trigger:** close below 154 with simultaneous global-equity declines, wider credit and AUD/JPY below 108.
- **Interpretation:** yen-funded deleveraging is the dominant risk channel; do not call benign normalisation.
- **Invalidation:** USD/JPY returns to 155–160 and risk assets stabilise.

### China demand confirmation

- **Upside warning:** copper >$6.75 and iron ore >$102 (or Dalian >CNY735).
- **Downside warning:** copper <$6.30 and iron ore <$92 (or Dalian <CNY690).
- **Trigger:** same-direction move persists two sessions and is supported by premiums/imports, steel margins/hot metal, inventories, credit or property data.
- **Invalidation:** commodities diverge or physical data contradict price.

### Product/LNG-led inflation shock

- **Warning:** active Brent < $100 while diesel cracks and TTF rise.
- **Trigger:** official inventory/refinery/LNG-flow data confirm shortage and inflation breakevens/policy pricing rise.
- **Interpretation:** consumer/producer inflation can strengthen without a crude breakout.
- **Invalidation:** refinery runs, product exports and LNG arrivals normalise while cracks/TTF fall.

### AI-concentration failure

- **Warning:** Nasdaq holds above 26,400 but equal-weight breadth, semis or credit deteriorate.
- **Trigger:** Nasdaq closes below 25,800 with weak breadth and US10Y >5.30%, or major AI-capex guidance is cut across more than one bellwether.
- **Interpretation:** the main offset to duration disorder has failed; reassess full broad risk-off.
- **Invalidation:** Nasdaq >27,200 with broader participation and stable credit.

## Physical confirmation checklist

**Energy:** Gulf loadings, tanker traffic, pipeline capacity, product exports, refinery runs, EIA inventories, Brent curve, cracks, TTF storage/LNG arrivals.  
**China/bulk:** hot metal, steel margins, port stocks, property sales/starts, TSF/credit, Simandou arrivals.  
**Transition metals:** exchange and bonded stocks, Chinese premiums/imports, Indonesian ore supply, lithium mine utilisation, utility uranium contracting.  
**Precious:** Treasury real yields, DXY, ETF flows, official purchases, lease rates and regional premiums.  
**Risk:** equal-weight breadth, semis/software participation, credit spreads, MOVE/VIX and funding markets.

## Data caveats

- Never compare expiring Brent November with active December without stating the roll.
- Do not mix LME and COMEX copper, or spot and futures precious-metal settlements, silently.
- Market-implied policy probabilities are provider/model dependent; compare like with like.
- `N/V` is preferable to an unrepeatable coking-coal or sector return.
- Real-yield confirmation uses the US Treasury/FRED close, not an intraday estimate.
