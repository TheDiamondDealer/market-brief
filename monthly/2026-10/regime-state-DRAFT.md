# Proposed Regime State — October 2026

> **AWAITING HUMAN APPROVAL.** Proposed replacement only. The live `regime-state.md` is unchanged.

**As of:** 30 September 2026 close  
**Classification:** **SHIFTING**  
**Verdict:** **ACUTE ENERGY AND LONG-DURATION STAGFLATION INTACT; DURATION DISORDER CONFIRMED; BROAD DISORDERLY RISK-OFF NOT FULLY RESTORED.**

## Executive state

September confirmed that the physical energy shock has migrated into global policy, real yields and term premium. Brent gained about 14%; the Federal Reserve, Bank of Japan and Reserve Bank of Australia all raised rates; the US 10-year Treasury yield rose 53 bp to 5.292%, and the 10-year real yield reached about 2.93%. Gold fell 6.6% and the ASX 200 fell roughly 3.2%, validating the rates and imported-inflation channels.

The regime is not yet a fully restored broad liquidation. The Nasdaq gained 1.86% for September, the S&P 500 fell only 0.45%, DXY ended 101.47 rather than decisively above 102, USD/JPY ended near 157.34 after the BOJ hike, and broad credit widening was not verified. AI capex/earnings, yen normalisation and late-month Gulf export recovery remain meaningful offsets.

## Active pillars

### 1. Physical energy stress — active, partially repaired

- Expiring Brent November settled $103.50 on 30 September; the more active December contract settled $98.03. Reuters calculated a roughly 14% monthly gain; WTI gained about 5% to $90.42.
- Middle East crude exports recovered materially late in September, but US–Iran talks stalled and tight diesel/fuel markets persisted.
- TTF remained near €72/MWh, preserving acute European/LNG inflation stress. Henry Hub ended near $3/mmBtu after briefly breaking above the former $3.10 range ceiling.
- OPEC+ completed the rollback of 1.65 mb/d of cuts in September but actual deliverability remains below nominal capacity; another roughly 2 mb/d cut layer lasts through year-end.

**Interpretation:** the shock is acute but two-sided. Physical repair can reduce the premium quickly; rhetoric without export/product confirmation cannot.

### 2. Policy and duration — active and escalated

- Fed: +25 bp to 3.75–4.00% on 16 September, 12–0.
- BOJ: +25 bp to 1.25% on 18 September, 7–2.
- RBA: +25 bp to 4.60% on 29 September, unanimous.
- US 10-year: 5.292%, +53 bp in September; 10-year real yield about 2.93%, versus 2.44% on 1 September; breakeven near 2.35%.
- Softer August PCE reduced October Fed-hike odds to about 37%, yet long yields still rose. Duration disorder is therefore not only a near-term policy-probability story.

**Interpretation:** the September 25 live amendment is validated and extended. Real yields/term premium are now a core regime pillar.

### 3. Strong dollar — repairing, not fully confirmed

- DXY ended near 101.47, up roughly 2% over the month and at the edge of the prior trigger.
- AUD/USD fell to about 0.6948; the Australian dollar absorbed domestic tightening, weak China breadth and high global yields.
- USD/CNY remained broadly stable and the yen strengthened on the month, preventing a clean one-way dollar-stress call.

**Interpretation:** promote the dollar from “invalidated” to “warning/repairing.” Full confirmation requires DXY >102 plus cross-FX and risk-asset breadth.

### 4. Equities and credit — divergent

- S&P 500: -0.45% in September; Nasdaq: +1.86%; ASX 200: about -3.2%.
- Nine of eleven S&P sectors fell on 30 September, and monthly constituent breadth was materially weaker than the cap-weighted index.
- Nasdaq resilience reflects AI concentration and cash-flow/capex support, not broad immunity to 5% yields.
- Broad credit widening was not verified in the completed monthly evidence set.

**Interpretation:** broad disorderly risk-off remains unconfirmed. Australia and equal-weight breadth show stress; US megacap technology still offsets it.

### 5. Precious metals — tactical rates channel restored

- Gold fell 6.6% to about $4,154/oz as real yields and DXY rose.
- Silver, platinum and palladium also ended lower; WPIC revised 2026 platinum to a 265 koz surplus.
- Central-bank demand remains structural: reported July buying was 23 t and China added about 20 t in August.

**Interpretation:** classify gold-versus-real-yields as **confirmed inverse**. Official demand is a medium-term support, not a daily safe-haven override.

### 6. China and transition materials — bifurcated

- Official manufacturing PMI returned to 50.1; production 51.7 and new orders 50.5. Employment remained 48.4.
- Non-manufacturing was 50.2, but new orders 46.5 and employment 45.9.
- Copper held around $6.55/lb; iron ore remained near $96.6/t; lithium fell toward CNY122.8k and nickel near $16k/t.
- Targeted PBOC/property support and a two-month US–China trade-truce extension reduce tail risk but do not establish broad reflation.

**Interpretation:** maintain a split China impulse. Do not promote copper strength into a broad-demand signal without property/steel/inventory confirmation.

### 7. Yen and carry — normalisation, not unwind

- BOJ raised rates to 1.25%; USD/JPY ended about 157.34, roughly 1.5% lower for September.
- AUD/JPY fell more sharply as the Australian dollar weakened.
- No simultaneous equity/credit liquidation was verified.

**Interpretation:** yen strength is a policy/intervention signal. A carry-unwind regime requires cross-asset deleveraging.

## Escalation, restoration and de-escalation rules

### Restore full disorderly risk-off if

- DXY closes above **102** and Nasdaq below **25,800** with weak breadth and observable credit widening; or
- USD/JPY falls below **154** from above 160 with simultaneous global equity declines, wider credit and lower AUD/JPY; or
- an equivalent multi-session equity/credit/funding liquidation cluster develops.

### Further escalate acute stagflation/duration disorder if

- Active-month Brent closes above **$110** with verified route/export/product impairment; or
- US 10-year sustains above **5.50%** and 10-year real yield above **3.00%**; or
- policy tightening produces simultaneous equity, credit and carry deterioration.

### De-escalate toward stabilisation if

- Active-month Brent closes below **$95** and then **$90** with verified flow repair;
- US 10-year closes below **5.00%** and then **4.80%**;
- DXY closes below **100.5**;
- equity breadth improves and credit remains contained.

### Break the regime only if

The de-escalation cluster persists and either global demand weakens enough to make policy easing credible, or physical energy conditions normalise enough that headline and core inflation pathways move lower across the US and Australia. One soft data print or one ceasefire headline is insufficient.

## Scenario probabilities

- **Base — managed acute stagflation with duration disorder: 45%.** Oil remains $95–110, long yields 5.0–5.5%, policy stays restrictive and US equity concentration survives.
- **Upside/inflationary — renewed physical escalation: 30%.** Oil >$110, DXY >102 and credit/equity/carry confirmation restore broad disorder.
- **Downside/disinflationary — flow repair and weak labour: 25%.** Oil < $95, US10Y <5.0%, Fed pause guidance and broader equity relief.
